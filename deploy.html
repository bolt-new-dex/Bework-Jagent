BEWORK LAUNCH CONSOLE

Owner-only deploy page for the new BEWORK system: $BGEM token → BEWORK PASS contract (free rounds + 5-hour Field claims) → fund the claim pool → open the mint. Each step is one signature in your wallet. Nothing here runs by itself.

Read first: deploy with the project OWNER wallet on BNB Smart Chain. Opening the mint (step 05) starts the clock for the free rounds and the Fields — do it only when you are ready to launch. You need roughly 0.01 BNB in the wallet for all the gas.

01 · Connect wallet

Connect the owner wallet. The console checks you are on BNB Smart Chain (56).

Not connected.

02 · Deploy $BGEM token

Bework Gem (BGEM) — fixed supply 1,000,000,000, all minted to your wallet at deploy. No mint, no burn, no tax, ever.

03 · Deploy BEWORK PASS

The new Pass contract: 3,333 supply · Agent ID only · 333 free every 3 hours (1 per wallet per round) · 0.002 BNB paid · max 12 per wallet · Fields every 5 hours with the $BGEM claim built in. Metadata base: https://bework.pro/nft/metadata/

04 · Fund the claim pool

Send 500,000,000 BGEM from your wallet into the Pass contract. That is the pool every Field claim is paid from.

05 · Open the mint

This flips the mint live and starts the clock: the first free round and Field Nº 1 begin at this exact moment. Only press it when the launch post is ready.

06 · Status

Live reads from both contracts.

When steps 02–05 are done, send both contract addresses to Muse — the homepage and skill.md get wired to them and the mint goes public. This page stores the addresses only in this browser (localStorage).
